Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Voidhenge is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, made by an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in and no tracking. The game has no analytics of its own.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • Tips and hint packs are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads are optional, and only some versions of the game have them. In those, an ad plays only when you choose to watch one for more hints. Google AdMob shows it, and it is never personalised. If the game offers no “Watch an ad” button, your version has no ads at all.

The game says it in a line of its own, under Settings ▸ About ▸ Privacy. In a version without ads: “No accounts, no adverts, no tracking. Progress and settings never leave this device.” In a version with ads: “No accounts. An advert plays only when you choose to watch one for hints, and Google AdMob shows it. Progress and settings never leave this device.”

What stays on your device

Your progress (the levels you have solved, your moves and stars, and your Daily Henge results), your settings, and the number of hints left on each board are stored only on your device, in the game’s own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Like any app’s data, they are included in your device’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer), and Quick Start moves them to a new device. Deleting the game deletes them from your device.

Purchases

Voidhenge is free to download. Inside the game you can buy two things, both through Apple’s App Store:

  • Hint packs: 10 more hints for the board you are playing.
  • Tips: an optional thank-you to the developer. A tip unlocks nothing.

Apple handles every purchase through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. The game receives only Apple’s confirmation that a purchase went through, so that it can add the hints or say thank you. Prices come from the App Store, in your own currency. Apple’s handling of your purchases is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Optional ads

Some versions of the game have optional ads, and some have none. In a version with ads, when a board’s free hints run out, you can choose to watch a short video ad for 5 more hints. That is the only place ads appear. They never play on their own, never appear between levels, and never appear anywhere else in the game. If that option is not there, ads are not available in your version of the game, and it contains no ad software at all.

The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. In versions where ads are available, Google’s ad software is part of the game. It keeps a little of its own data on your device, and each time the game starts it checks whether the internet can be reached by looking up a Google web address. It requests no ad and sends Google none of the information below until you tap to watch one. If you never do, no ad is ever requested.

When you choose to watch an ad

  • Where the law asks for consent (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google’s consent form appears first. You can change your choice later under Settings ▸ About ▸ Ad privacy choices.
  • To deliver the ad, Google’s software receives information from your device. Google describes it as your IP address (which can show your general location), app performance and crash information, which ads you see and how you interact with them, and an identifier that is limited to this developer’s apps.
  • The game never asks for permission to track you, so Google cannot use your device’s advertising identifier.
  • The game asks Google for non-personalised ads only, suitable for all ages. Google may still use the information above to limit how often you see an ad, to measure ads in total and to prevent fraud.

Google’s use of this information is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google is the only company the game shares any information with, and only in a version with ads, when you choose to watch one. Google receives it under the AdMob terms and its own privacy policy, which bind it to protect that information at least as well as this policy does. How long Google keeps it, and how Google deletes it, is set out in How Google retains data we collect. The data Google’s software keeps on your device is deleted when you delete the game.

Sharing a picture

If you press Share on a results card, the game hands a picture of the finished board, and one line about it, to your device’s share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice, and the game keeps no record of it. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks your permission to add the picture to Photos. The game never reads your photo library.

What the game connects to

The game connects to the internet only through Apple’s App Store, to show prices and to complete a purchase you start, and, in versions with ads, through Google’s ad software: the check described above when the game starts, and the ad itself if you choose to watch one. Everything else works offline. The Daily Henge comes from your device’s own date.

Children

Voidhenge is suitable for everyone. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children. In versions with ads, watching one sends Google the information listed under “When you choose to watch an ad”. On a child’s device, Apple’s Ask to Buy and Screen Time settings control purchases, and any ad is limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Every level can be played without buying anything or watching an ad. Hints are optional help.
  • You can reset your progress in Settings ▸ Reset, or erase all of the game’s data by deleting it.
  • To share nothing with Google, don’t tap “Watch an ad”: no ad is requested, and none of the information listed under “When you choose to watch an ad” is sent to Google, until you do.
  • Where it applies, you can change your ad consent under Settings ▸ About ▸ Ad privacy choices. The row is there wherever Google’s consent rules call for it, and you can withdraw your consent there at any time.
  • To ask Google to see or delete information it holds, use the choices Google’s Privacy Policy describes. Deleting the game removes Google’s software, and the data it kept, from your device.
  • If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, use Settings ▸ About ▸ Report an ad, or email us (see Contact), and we will report it to Google.

Because we collect no personal information, we hold nothing about you to access, correct, delete, sell or share. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you, and we don’t keep them longer than needed.
