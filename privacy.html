Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Voidhenge is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, made by an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game collects no personal information, so it has none to pass on to anyone.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • Tips and hint packs are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details.

The game sums it up in a line of its own, under Settings ▸ About ▸ Privacy.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the levels you have solved, your moves and stars, and your Daily Henge results), your settings, and the number of hints left on each board are stored only on your device, in the game’s own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Like any app’s data, they are included in your device’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer), and Quick Start moves them to a new device. Deleting the game deletes them from your device.

Purchases

Voidhenge is free to download. Inside the game you can buy two things, both through Apple’s App Store:

  • Hint packs: 10 more hints for the board you are playing.
  • Tips: an optional thank-you to the developer. A tip unlocks nothing.

Apple handles every purchase through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. The game receives only Apple’s confirmation that a purchase went through, so that it can add the hints or say thank you. Prices come from the App Store, in your own currency. Apple’s handling of your purchases is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

No third-party SDKs, analytics or tracking

The game is built with the open-source Godot Engine, which collects nothing, and it contains no third-party SDKs and no analytics. It does not track you, and it never asks for permission to track you. Nothing about how you play is sent anywhere.

Sharing a picture

If you press Share on a results card, the game hands a picture of the finished board, and one line about it, to your device’s share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice, and the game keeps no record of it. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks your permission to add the picture to Photos. The game never reads your photo library.

What the game connects to

The game connects to the internet only through Apple’s App Store, to show prices and to complete a purchase you start. Everything else works offline. The Daily Henge comes from your device’s own date.

Children

Voidhenge is suitable for everyone. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. On a child’s device, Apple’s Ask to Buy and Screen Time settings control purchases.

Your choices

  • Every level can be played without buying anything. Hints are optional help.
  • You can reset your progress in Settings ▸ Reset, or erase all of the game’s data by deleting it.

Because we collect no personal information, we hold nothing about you to access, correct, delete, sell or share. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you, and we don’t keep them longer than needed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
