Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, on iOS 15.0 or later. The game is played upright (on iPad, either way up). A keyboard or a game controller can play every screen.

Where is my progress kept?

On your device, in the game’s own storage. It is saved the moment you solve a level and after every move, so closing the app mid-level brings you back to the same board.

Your progress goes with your device’s own backup, like any app’s data. A new iPhone or iPad set up from an iCloud or computer backup, or with Quick Start, brings it along. There is no account and no sync between two devices, so a level solved on your iPad does not appear on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes its progress.

Starting over: Settings ▸ Reset ▸ Reset all progress… (it asks first) clears your progress and keeps your settings. Restore default settings… does the opposite.

How do hints work?

Every board, each level and each Daily Henge, starts with 10 free hints. Press Hint and the board points to the peg to move. Press it again and it shows which way to turn it. That uses one hint. Being shown an answer you have already paid for costs nothing, until you solve the board.

A hint never takes a star away, but a level solved with one is noted on the results card, and its stars are drawn hollow. Solve a level with three stars and no hint to make it perfect.

When a board’s hints run out, Hint offers to Buy 10 hints for it at the price shown, which comes from the App Store in your currency, as many times as you like. Not now closes the offer and leaves the board as it was.

Extra hints belong to the board you got them for, and they stay if you leave and come back. When you solve that board, it goes back to 10 free hints and any extra hints you didn’t use are gone, so it is worth getting only what you need. A Daily Henge’s extra hints also end when its day does, and each new day’s board starts with 10 free hints.

Stuck without hints? Undo takes back a move, Restart puts the board back as it began, and the pause menu’s “How it works” explains every mechanism you have met so far.

What is the Daily Henge?

A new board each day, the same for everyone, chosen from your device’s own date, with no connection needed. It is on the main menu from the very start, beside Chapters, and the results card counts how many days in a row you have solved one. A day’s board may use a mechanism you have not met yet: the pause menu’s “How it works” explains every one on that board.

Purchases and the Tip jar

Voidhenge is free. You can buy hint packs when a board runs out of hints, and leave an optional tip in Settings ▸ Tip jar. Tips come in six sizes, from $1 to $100 or the equivalent in your currency. A tip unlocks nothing; it is simply a thank-you, and it is very much appreciated.

  • A purchase was interrupted. If the app closed or the connection dropped, the purchase is completed the next time you open the game.
  • Waiting for approval. With Ask to Buy, a purchase goes through once a parent approves it.
  • Restoring. Hint packs and tips are consumables: the App Store cannot restore them, and they are not shared between your devices. Hints you have not used yet go with your device’s backup, like the rest of your progress.
  • Refunds. Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The game is silent.

Voidhenge follows your Ring/Silent switch, like the system’s own sounds, so switch it to ring. Music and effects each have a slider under Settings ▸ Sound. While another app is playing audio, the game’s music steps aside and its effects carry on; the music returns when the other audio stops.

Accessibility

Settings ▸ Accessibility has Reduced motion, High contrast, Colour-blind mode (every crystal and its seat also have their own shape, so colour is never the only cue) and Read aloud, which speaks each screen, the control you move to and every move in your device’s voice.

Settings ▸ Display has Interface size (Standard, Larger or Largest), Brighter board for bright daylight or a dimmed screen, Keep screen awake, and Battery saver, which caps the frame rate at 30. On first launch the game follows your device’s Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast and text size, and keeps following them unless you choose otherwise in the game.

Sharing a solved board

Press Share on the results card to send a picture of the finished board, with the level and your stars, through your device’s share sheet. Choosing Save Image there asks once for permission to add the picture to Photos.

Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, and your progress and settings never leave your device. Read the full privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

Email cartryder29@gmail.com with your device, its iOS version, the game’s version (Settings ▸ About ▸ Version) and, if you can, the level and what you did just before. A screenshot helps.
